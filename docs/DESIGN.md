## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined Mediterranean sporting heritage rooted in 1974, harmonized with high-utility digital agility. The visual tone bridges artisanal court craft with crisp modernism: the tactile warmth of sun-baked red clay (*terra batida*), the structural authority of deep Catalan cobalt, and the dynamic tension of high-visibility optic tennis felt (*chartreuse*).

### Personality & Tone
- **Atmospheric Heritage:** Reverence for club culture, physical clay courts, and historical longevity without appearing dusty or dated.
- **Sporting Elegance:** Confident, structured typography paired with editorial breathing room, reminiscent of vintage 1970s European tennis club periodicals and tournament brochures.
- **Utilitarian Clarity:** High contrast for effortless booking flows, membership management, and court reservation timetables under direct sunlight.

### Design Movement
**Editorial Modernism with Tactile Sporting Heritage.** The system avoids generic tech software clichés by utilizing intentional grid layouts, warm architectural off-whites, crisp geometric borders, and graphic court-line motifs.

## Colors

The palette balances tournament-grade sport energy with the natural materials of the Catalan Urgell landscape.

### Palette Architecture
- **Cobalt Blue (`#1434CB`):** Primary brand anchor. Represents structural confidence, club authority, and clear administrative hierarchy. Used for hero blocks, high-emphasis states, and primary navigation active markers.
- **Terra Batida (`#C85A32`):** Secondary heritage accent. Evokes crushed brick and red clay surfaces. Applied to court status badges, vintage date stamps ("Des de 1974"), architectural isometric court illustrations, and warm focal callouts.
- **Optic Tennis Yellow (`#D2F23A`):** Tertiary electric accent. An energetic chartreuse that punctures neutral grounds. Used for ticker banners, active selection states, micro-interactive badges, and key high-conversion actions.
- **Alabaster Sand (`#F6F4EE`):** Primary canvas neutral. A warm, sunlit off-white surface replacing sterile digital gray, providing natural warmth and reducing glare.
- **Court Lines White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated card containers, floating reservation panels, and crisp contrast overlays.
- **Charcoal Basalt (`#12161A`):** Deep editorial neutral for sharp typographic hierarchy and borders.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric authority of 1970s sporting poster design with contemporary clarity.

### Family Pairing
- **Headlines & Display (`Space Grotesk`):** A striking sans-serif with tight geometric shapes, subtle technical quirks, and European editorial punch. Delivers a modernist athletic identity that honors the club's 1974 founding.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, approachable, and legible across dense booking matrices, timetables, and mobile screens.
- **Eyebrows & Archival Tags (`Space Grotesk Uppercase`):** Letterspaced with generous tracking (`0.12em`) to frame dates, category chips, and section coordinates like "DES DE 1974" and "TERRA BATIDA".

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid system anchors the editorial perspective while preserving clean dashboard functionality.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` side gutters. Maximum container width capped at `1280px` for optimal reading measure. Split panels present visual graphic representations (court diagrams, match timelines) alongside reservation tools.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1rem` gutters and `2rem` side margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Content reflows into single column vertical stacks, transforming side-by-side court visualizers into carousel-ready cards.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere to generous rhythm intervals (`space-2xl` for homepage editorial chapters, `space-lg` for transactional forms and court booking steps).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes flat planes, warm layering, and crisp boundaries inspired by pristine tennis court striping rather than deep artificial drop shadows.

### Elevation Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Sandy Mediterranean field (`#F6F4EE`), establishing ground temperature.
- **Layer 1 (Card Level):** Crisp court white (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border `1px solid rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.08)` and an ambient, low-slung shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Popovers & Selected Courts):** Elevated booking modals and date pickers use a precise dual shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.04)` with crisp court-line boundary borders.
- **Architectural Overlays:** Heritage numbers (e.g. large transparent "1974" or court numbers "03") sit directly behind court illustrations at `12%` opacity, establishing graphic depth without elevation.

## Shapes

The geometry uses a measured 8px baseline curvature (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the rounded corners of classical tennis rackets and service boundary boxes while retaining structural discipline.

### Corner Archetypes
- **Standard UI Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Small Badges):** `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) for a comfortable, modern hand-feel.
- **Reservation & Content Containers:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) ensuring self-contained module boundaries.
- **Pill Tags & Action Switches:** `rounded-full` (`9999px`) exclusively reserved for court-type tags (*Terra Batida*, *Quick*), match score counters, and quick filter toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Court Booking & Key Confirmation):** Deep Cobalt Blue (`#1434CB`) background, pure white label, `0.5rem` radius, with subtle 1px border `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`. Hover shifts to `#0D2A99` with smooth `150ms` ease.
- **Secondary Action (Club Membership / Explore):** High-voltage Tennis Chartreuse (`#D2F23A`) with Charcoal text (`#12161A`). Hover brings micro-lift of `-1px` and soft chartreuse glow.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Hairline Charcoal border (`rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.2)`), sand background, accompanied by upward-angled arrow `↗` for external or modal flows.

### Court Selection Chips & Filters
- **Inactive:** Background white, border `1px solid rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.1)`, muted text.
- **Active:** Deep Cobalt `#1434CB` or Terracota `#C85A32` background with white Space Grotesk typography, featuring a micro court dot.

### Inputs & Date-Time Pickers
- **Field Anatomy:** Minimum touch height `48px`. Pure white background framed with a crisp `1px solid rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.14)`. Active focus shifts border to `#1434CB` with an instant, clean 2px focus ring (`rgba(20, 52, 203, 0.15)`).
- **Time Slots:** Modular tiles showing hour intervals (e.g., `08:00 - 09:30`). Available slots display in crisp white with subtle border; reserved slots render in muted diagonal hatched or tinted neutral states.

### Cards
- **Court Schedule Card:** Clean white card wrapped in subtle border, featuring court surface tag (Terra Batida in terracota pill), duration selector, and live price/member status.
- **Editorial Archive Card:** Sand background, historical 1974 timeline node with vertical connection rail, typography in Space Grotesk.

### Club Marquee Ticker
- Continuous looping banner filled with Tennis Lime (`#D2F23A`) running black uppercase Space Grotesk slogans separated by eight-pointed athletic star motifs (`✳ CLUB TENNIS AGRAMUNT ✳ TENNIS DE SEMPRE. NOVES HISTÒRIES. ✳ 1974 ✳`).
